# My Desk Setup 2026

After years of iteration, I've finally settled on a setup that balances aesthetics with productivity.

## The Essentials

### 🖥️ Display
**LG 27" 4K Monitor** - Crystal clear text rendering for long coding sessions. The extra screen real estate is a game-changer when switching between code, docs, and browser.

### ⌨️ Keyboard
**Keychron K2 (Brown Switches)** - The perfect balance between tactile feedback and quiet operation. Wireless connectivity means I can quickly switch between my laptop and desktop.

**Why I love it:**
- Hot-swappable switches for future customization
- Mac and Windows compatible
- Compact 75% layout saves desk space
- Amazing battery life (3+ weeks)

### 🖱️ Mouse
**Logitech MX Master 3** - Ergonomic design that prevents wrist strain. The infinite scroll wheel is perfect for navigating long documents and code files.

### 🎧 Audio
**Sony WH-1000XM5** - Best noise cancellation for deep focus. The ambient mode is great for quick conversations without removing them.

### 💡 Lighting
**BenQ ScreenBar** - No more screen glare or eye strain. This desk lamp clips onto the monitor and provides asymmetric lighting.

## The Philosophy

My setup follows three principles:

1. **Minimal Distractions** - Only what I need, nothing more
2. **Ergonomic First** - Comfort enables productivity
3. **Quality Over Quantity** - Buy once, use for years

## What's Missing

I'm still looking for:
- A good standing desk converter
- Better cable management solution
- Acoustic treatment for video calls
